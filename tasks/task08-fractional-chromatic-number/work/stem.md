Customers arrive at a single-server queue as a Poisson process with rate 0.5 per unit time and are served one at a time in order of arrival. Service times are independent of each other and of the arrivals, with the Weibull survival function $P(S > t) = \exp(-(t/\eta)^\beta)$ for $t \ge 0$, where $\eta > 0$ and $\beta > 0$ are not given. The figure shows $P(S > t)$ on logarithmic axes. The curve is drawn so that it passes exactly through gridline crossings at two of the $t$ values labelled on the axis, one of them $t = 3$; reading those two crossings fixes $\eta$ and $\beta$.

Using the $\eta$ and $\beta$ fixed by the figure, find the standard deviation of the waiting time in queue (the time from arrival until service starts) of a customer when the queue is in steady state, to two significant figures. Give only this number as the answer.
