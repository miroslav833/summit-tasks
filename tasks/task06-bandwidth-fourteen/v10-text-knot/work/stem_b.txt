A structure prediction model returned a model of a $184$-residue protein. To examine its topology, the $C_\alpha$ trace was coarse-grained (each helix replaced by its axis, each strand and loop by a smooth curve through its residues) and projected onto a plane. The direction of projection was chosen so that the straight segment joining the two termini crosses no part of the chain; that segment is added to close the chain into a closed curve, so the closed curve consists of the chain from residue $1$ to residue $184$ followed by the segment back to residue $1$.

In the projection the chain crosses itself exactly $12$ times. Every crossing is listed in the table below by the two residues at which the two parts of the chain meet: the residue on the part in front (over) and the residue on the part behind (under). The chain is oriented from residue $1$ to residue $184$. The sign of a crossing is $+1$ when the direction of the under strand is obtained from the direction of the over strand by a counterclockwise rotation of less than $180$ degrees, and $-1$ otherwise. There are no other crossings.

The closed curve is a knot. What is the minimal crossing number of this knot, that is, the smallest number of crossings in any diagram of its knot type? Give a single integer.
