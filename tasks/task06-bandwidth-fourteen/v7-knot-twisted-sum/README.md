Design: the closed backbone is the connected sum 5_1 # 6_3 drawn as an alternating diagram with twelve crossings. Eleven of them are the standard reduced alternating diagrams of 5_1 (a five-crossing twist) and 6_3 (a closed 3-braid, blocks 2,1,1,2); the twelfth is a half twist in the neck that joins the two parts. That neck crossing is nugatory (the arc leaving it returns to it without crossing anything else), so the diagram is alternating but not reduced. Tait's rule "alternating diagram, count the crossings" is the fast answer and gives 12; it only holds for reduced diagrams. Removing the twist gives an eleven-crossing reduced alternating diagram, and by Kauffman, Murasugi and Thistlethwaite the crossing number is 11. Key: 11. Expected wrong answer: 12. Other wrong answers: 10 (removing a second crossing by mistake), 6 or 5 (a summand only).
